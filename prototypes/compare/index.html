Same quiz, two builds

Tap the letter for the note. Miss once and you get one more try. Miss twice and the answer stays up until you tap Continue. Both builds share the same quiz logic; only the drawing and animation differ.

Phaser 4 WebGL canvas, game engine

What to compare

  • Sharpness. Pinch-zoom into each staff. SVG stays crisp at any zoom; the canvas is drawn for your screen and softens when zoomed past it.
  • Feedback feel. Get one right and one wrong in each. Phaser's spark burst, flash and shake come from the engine; the Svelte version recreates them with a few lines of GSAP.
  • Taps. Try fast answers on your iPad. Both should register instantly with no zoom on double-tap.
  • Size. The numbers above are what each version adds to the offline download.
