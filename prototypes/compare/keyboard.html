Find it on the keyboard

Read the note, then tap that key. Miss once and you get one more try; miss twice and the right key lights up until you tap Continue. On a phone, swipe the keyboard sideways to reach other octaves.

Things to try

  • Octave must match on: only the exact key counts (C6 is not C5). Off: any key with the right letter counts.
  • Both clefs: the keyboard widens to the full range, G1 to F6. Middle C has a teal dot.
  • Listen with mic: plays from your real piano count as taps. This preview page may block the microphone; the installed app will ask for permission instead.
